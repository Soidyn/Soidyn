Soidyn Studio

Real-time spatial audio engine for Mac

Turn any audio on your Mac into a real-time 3D surround experience — no extra speakers needed, just your headphones. Soidyn Studio captures audio directly from your Mac's system output and re-renders it through HRTF (Head-Related Transfer Function) processing, creating the sensation of sound coming from all around you — front, rear, and even overhead.

What makes it different

  • Auto 3D Object Tracker — sound objects automatically shift position based on content, no manual panning required
  • 9-Channel Virtual Speaker Array — including 4 dedicated height channels for true overhead presence
  • Auto Bass Booster & Dialog Enhancement — consistently punchy bass with dialogue that stays clear, even in busy mixes
  • Built-in Parametric EQ — fine-tune your sound via a simple config file
  • Real-time processing — perfect for movies, gaming, or music on the go

Built for

Audio enthusiasts, competitive gamers who rely on positional audio cues, content creators, and anyone who wants a serious headphone upgrade without buying new hardware.

Requirements

macOS · BlackHole (free virtual audio driver) for audio routing
